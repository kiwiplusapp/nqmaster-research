Current NQMaster and GoldMaster logic replayed on real MNQ and MGC futures from 1 Feb 2024 to 25 Sep 2026: 657 trading days, the whole real-futures history we have. So "the last 3 years" here is 2.7 years. Costs are $1.90 per round turn plus 1 tick of slippage per side. Every figure is for 1 contract per module unless a row says otherwise.

The short answer

MyFundedFutures Rapid EOD 50K/Profile Ultra, eval and funded/MNQ + MGC/1 contract

Use MyFundedFutures first. Its funded account pays out everything above a $2,100 buffer every day with no cap. With the same strategy, it makes roughly 40–50% more per account than Lucid. You can hold 3 Rapid accounts there. Lucid Flex 50K is a good second firm: same profile, slightly faster eval, up to 5 funded accounts.

Ultra beats WR70Plus for money. WR70Plus wins more trades (69.8% vs 65.2%) and passes more evals (83% vs 77%). But it takes 8–10 more trading days to pass and earns about 40% less once funded. Use WR70Plus only if a high trade win rate matters more to you than income.

How sure I am: about 80% that the edge is real and the system makes money over a year. It will probably earn less than the backtest. Plan on roughly half: about $1,050 per account per month at MFFU with 1 contract, or about $1,580 with the 2-contract step-up. Details below.

3 · Money

Money per month, per account

Each simulation runs one account for 12 months. It pays for an eval, trades until it passes, collects payouts while funded, and buys a new eval after a bust or the last payout. The figures are net of eval fees and the 10% firm share, averaged over all 12-month windows that start every third trading day. "+1 tick" doubles slippage to 2 ticks per side. "Losing years" is the share of resampled years that end below zero.

The 2-contract step-up is a manual rule. At MyFundedFutures, trade 1 contract until the balance reaches $53,100, which leaves $3,000 above the locked drawdown limit. Then switch NQMaster and GoldMaster to Contracts 2 and withdraw only above $56,100. Go back to 1 contract if the balance falls below $53,100. At Lucid the 2-contract rule adds little and quadruples funded busts (2.6 vs 0.6 per year), so I don't recommend it there.

4 · Setup

Instruments and exact settings

Run two charts on the same account: NQMaster on MNQ and GoldMaster on MGC. Both use 1-minute bars over the full Globex session (18:00–17:00 ET). Use the front-month contract and roll on NinjaTrader's roll date. Every other market tested has been rejected: S&P (MES), oil, euro and silver. Their costs eat the edge.

MNQMicro E-mini Nasdaq-100 · tick 0.25 = $0.50 · $2 per point

NQMaster, Profile Ultra. Makes about 87% of the profit. On a 1-contract setting it opens at most about 11 micros at once (99% of the time 7 or fewer).

MGCMicro Gold · tick 0.10 = $1.00 · $10 per point

GoldMaster, Robust for Ultra accounts and WinRate for WR70Plus or Estable. It adds about $420 a month per contract and moves independently of NQ (correlation 0.00). That smooths the equity curve.

Settings not listed stay at their defaults. In particular: ORB prior-close filter 0.10, VOLB break-even on, MOM agreement on, VOLB trend last entry 1047, Skip FOMC on, Min ATR 150, AdaptiveSize off, DailyLossLimit 0, EdgeMonitor on. At MyFundedFutures the eval also requires 4 trading days. If NQMaster reaches the $3,000 target earlier (rare at 1 contract), place one micro trade on each remaining day.

5 · Confidence

How sure I am, realistically

These percentages are my judgment. They start from the simulations above and then discount for what simulations cannot see: the future market regime, firm rule changes and live execution.

Why I believe the edge is real

  • NinjaTrader's own Strategy Analyzer reproduces the research. WR70Plus over the last 365 days: 837 trades at 68% WR in NinjaTrader vs 818 trades at 69.8% in research. GoldMaster: 58 trades, WR 74%, PF 2.59 vs 67 trades, 74.6%, 2.56. The older MaxSharpe profile over 2024–26: PF 1.31 vs 1.35.
  • These are real futures with real costs. Every year is positive (Ultra + gold PF 1.33 in 2024, 1.47 in 2025, 1.70 in 2026), and 26 of 32 months made money.
  • It still works with 4 extra ticks of slippage per side (PF stays at or above 1.30). It passes the deflated-Sharpe test that corrects for the 300,000+ configurations we mined.
  • The profit comes from about 20 modules that barely move together. No single one carries the result; the largest, VOLB, is $641 of the $3,143 a month.

Why I can't promise it

  • The modules were found while looking at 2020–2026, so none of this data is truly unseen. Your first live month is the first real out-of-sample test.
  • In 2015–2019 the market was calmer: the daily range was under 150 points most days. The same modules lost money after costs. If that regime returns, the edge shrinks toward zero. The Min ATR 150 guard stops trading on those days, which protects the account but also stops the income.
  • Choppy range days are the weak spot (PF 0.74–0.85). A month full of them loses: the worst month was −$3,132 per contract (September 2024), and August 2026 lost $2,306 with WR70Plus.
  • 2026 was exceptional at $5,206 a month. Don't extrapolate it; 2024 made $1,802 a month.
  • Firms change rules: Apex banned bots in March 2026. If MyFundedFutures reads its news rule as "every data release", Ultra loses 15–18% of its profit. MyFundedFutures can also move a funded account to live at its discretion, which ends the uncapped daily payouts.
  • Every account trades the same signals, so a bad month hits all of them at once.
  • The latest code changes (ORB prior-close filter, VOLB break-even, MOM agreement, news blackout) have not been compiled or tested in NinjaTrader yet.

How to find out quickly (the first 60 trading days)

  1. Compile NQMaster and GoldMaster in NinjaTrader (F5). Run the Strategy Analyzer from 2024-02-01 to today with the settings in section 4, starting the data 4–6 months earlier for warm-up. Send me the trades CSV and I'll compare it with this research trade by trade.
  2. Ask MyFundedFutures in writing: are automated NinjaTrader strategies allowed on Rapid accounts, and which releases count as Tier-1 news?
  3. Add the 2027 CPI and jobs-report times to NewsTimes before January.
  4. Start with one MyFundedFutures eval, with the edge monitor on. Add the second and third accounts after the first one passes.
  5. Stop adding accounts and review if live P&L per contract is below zero after 60 trading days or the edge monitor alarms. If the edge is real, each of those has less than a 10% chance of happening.

Method. The eval and funded simulations use minute-by-minute equity: realised P&L plus the open position's worst point in each minute. That checks the trailing drawdown the way the firm does. MyFundedFutures funded accounts apply the Tier-1 news blackout. FOMC days are skipped in every profile. Account-level daily profit stops: $800 in the MyFundedFutures eval (30% rule) and $1,400 at Lucid (50% rule). Not modelled: platform or data outages, firm discretion, and slippage beyond the +1 tick stress test.
