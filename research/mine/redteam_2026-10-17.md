## Verdict

- **"$250-300/day needs ~8 funded seats"** survives. At full edge 8 seats give a median of ~$290-460/day (2020-23 / 2024-26). At the
  realistic 35% haircut only "3 MFFU + 5 Lucid 150K" reaches it, about half the time (median $192-264 Ultra+gold, $236-326 with the six-year
  modules; P(>= $250) 34-62%). "3 MFFU + 5 Lucid 50K 2c" gives $136-228 (table P1). The T1 history column of the guide's tables is
  inflated 15-25% (H2); the bootstrap medians the guide quotes are not affected by the weighting, and the gold tick changes them by 1-5%.
- **"$200/day per account only via the MFFU ladder at full edge"** survives only as a **steady-state (year 2+) figure on the 2024-26 market,
  with Ultra + six-year modules, and only if the ladder is configured without the `Mffu50K` preset** (H1). First-year income is ~$145-160/day
  (2024-26) and ~$95-120 (2020-23); with a parameter choice made on 2020-23 only, ~15-19% lower and ~3x the busts; at 35% less edge
  $50-90/day. With the preset as shipped the ladder never exceeds ~$145/day even in steady state.

## HIGH

### H1. The `Mffu50K` preset silently overrides the ladder's payout level (implementation, `NQMaster.cs:206`)
`else { ... FundedPayoutAt = FundedStepUpCushion > 0 ? 6600 : 4600; MaxOpenContracts = 30; ... }` runs in DataLoaded whenever
`PropPreset = Mffu50K` and `PropMode = Funded`. The ladder recipe (guide section 2b: `FundedStepUpCushion 3000`, `FundedLadderStep 3000`,
`FundedLadderMax 4`, `FundedPayoutAt 11600`) does not say `PropPreset Manual`, so with the MFFU preset the account withdraws down to $6,100
(B = 6.1k): the day-start cushion after any payout is ~6,000 -> 3 contracts, 4 only between payouts. Re-simulated (same paths, 21-day block
bootstrap, 300 x 3-year sequences):

| Ultra+six+gold, full edge | yr-1 mean T1/T2 | steady (yrs 2-3) mean T1/T2 | P(steady >= $200) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ladder K4 S3k B11k as simulated | 130 / 173 | 205 / 252 | 51% / 77% |
| same with preset payout 6,600 (B 6.1k) | 88 / 112 | 123 / 145 | 1% / 15% |

Fix: in the preset, skip the `FundedPayoutAt` assignment when `FundedLadderStep > 0` (or set it to `(FundedLadderMax-1)*FundedLadderStep + 2600`),
and/or state `PropPreset Manual` + every preset field in the recipe.

### H2. Linear history windows overweight the middle of each period; T1 "history" is inflated (`seats_plan.py:125`, `ladder_plan.py:142`)
Starts every 5 days with the whole 252-day window inside the period means a day in mid-2021/2022 is in up to 50 windows and a day in
Feb-2020 or late-2023 in 1-2. Window-weighted mean daily P&L per contract (funded mix a+4b) vs the uniform period mean:

| set | T1 | T2 |
|---|---|---|
| Ultra+gold | 112.4 vs 95.9 (**+17.3%**) | 150.7 vs 143.6 (+5.0%) |
| Ultra+six+gold | 124.8 vs 109.7 (**+13.7%**) | 155.2 vs 149.8 (+3.6%) |

The haircut subtracts the uniform mean, so the history rows keep a residual edge: at "edge -100%" history still shows +$5..+49/day
(seats_plan T1) because ~$15-17/day per contract of edge remains (2022 is overweighted). Uniform-weight replacement (circular 252-day
windows over the period, same ordering): MFFU ladder K4 S3k B11k T1 169 -> 137 (Ultra+six+gold), 140 -> 110 (Ultra+gold); T2 221 -> 191,
196 -> 161. MFFU 1c T1 47 -> 37.

The guide's explanation ("la historia da más porque las rachas buenas se encadenan") is not what drives it: block length barely matters
(MFFU ladder K4 S3k B11k, Ultra+six+gold, full edge, T1/T2 bootstrap mean: block 1 day 124/168, 10 days 120/174, 21 days 121/183,
63 days 130/181). The gap history > circular comes from window weighting; circular > bootstrap from year-to-year edge dispersion
(each circular year carries its own mean; the account payoff is convex in the mean). A forward year's edge dispersion belongs in the
haircut prior, so the best single estimate is the bootstrap (block 21-63) or the circular distribution, **never the linear-history mean
for T1**. Bootstrap medians are lower than means because years are left-skewed in T2 (summer 2026) and right-skewed in T1.

### H3. Ladder parameters, six-year modules and gold were chosen with both periods in view (selection)
- **Ladder (K, S, B)**: choosing on T1 only (hist mean, bootstrap median or bootstrap mean all agree) picks **K4 S2k B8k**, not K4 S3k B11k.
  Its T2 (ladder_plan.json): Ultra+six+gold bootstrap median 154 vs 189 (**-19%**), P(>= $200) 33% vs 46%, funded busts **1.95/yr vs 0.69**;
  Ultra+gold 151 vs 175, busts 1.89 vs 0.64; steady state (yrs 2-3, T2) 194 vs 243. The guide's "the surface is flat" holds for the median
  $ but not for busts (x2.8). The ex-post T2 optimum is on the grid edge (K4 S4k B14k), so the grid did not bracket the optimum.
- **B11k is fitted to the single worst T2 day**: worst 1-contract day low in T2 = -$2,571/-$2,572 (T1 worst -$1,285). At 4 contracts that is
  -$10.3k, just inside the $11k cushion B11k leaves at 4c; B8k/B9k bust on it. Days per year with a 1c low <= -2,000: T1 0, T2 1.1-1.5;
  <= -2,250: T2 0.4-0.7. A forward day 10% worse than the sample maximum (ATR is a regime variable) busts the K4 account and its ~$11k
  retained balance.
- **Six-year modules**: chosen on T1 2020-10..2023 and gated on T2 (`six_lib` protocol needs T2 PF >= 1.2), default **OFF** in NQMaster,
  and they failed the account criterion on 2026-10-16 (six_life: T2 P(neg yr) 0 -> 9%, p10 557 -> 159). Today the guide calls Ultra+six+gold
  "el set recomendado". Their contribution to the ladder headline: +7% T2, +20% T1 (corrected steady state 232 vs 217 T2, 197 vs 163 T1);
  none of it is out of sample.
- **Gold Robust**: its modules were selected on the CFD 2020-23 that is T1 here; T2 is real MGC 2024-26, gold's best era on record
  (Databento 16y: Robust real PF 1.13/0.92/1.08/1.33 by block). Gold adds ~+$5-10/day per contract here; treat as optimistic.
- Ultra itself was assembled with 2020-26 in view (earlier red teams); "full edge" in both periods is in-sample. The project's own
  realistic case is the 35% haircut.

### M5. Firm "go live" rules not modelled
- **Lucid**: "when moved live ALL sim accounts close" (art 16558826, propfirms_2026.md); Flex moves to live after the 5th payout. The model
  just restarts the graduating seat. Joint 5-seat simulation (rt/lucid_live.py): P(at least one seat graduates within the year) at full edge
  86-100%, at -35% 50-94%, so it happens almost every year. Effect on 5 x Lucid Flex 50K plans: -3..-6% if the other seats are reset (their
  balances, ~$5-12k per year, are lost; fee refunded for funded with 0 payouts) and the live account earns nothing; +5..+15% if the live
  account keeps trading at the same size with daily payouts. Small in $, but the plan "5 Lucid seats running in parallel" is disrupted
  about once a year.
- **MFFU**: auto-live at a $10k single-day profit (1 day in 6.6 years at K4: 1c max day $2.7-3.7k), live at risk-team discretion after
  consistent payouts, and "live+sim can't be traded simultaneously, Rapid accounts merged into one live". The ladder (daily payouts of
  ~$200, $11k retained) is the profile most likely to be moved; not quantifiable, not mentioned in the guide.

## Checked and correct

- `+1 tick per side` (`cc` = contracts traded per day x $1) is applied once per gear and scaled by k everywhere: eval x8 lots
  (`ca + 8*cb`), Lucid 2c (`2*(ca + 4*cb)`), Lucid 150K 6c, the step-up `dbl` gear and the ladder gears (`k * full`). The base trades already
  carry 1 tick on market entries and stop/time exits (wrq_lib.xsim); the sims add one more.
- Haircut `h` sits inside `k * (...)`: scaled with size in every gear (the 2026-10-16 bug is not present).
- Eval sizes match NQMaster's EvalHighWrMultiplier rule (total MOM1030/CPI09 lots = max(4 x base, 8)): MFFU 1c 8, Lucid 1c 8,
  Lucid 2c 2 x 4 = 8, Lucid 150K 6 x 4 = 24.
- Ladder: NQMaster `LadderQty` = `FundedStepUpContracts + floor((cushion - FundedStepUpCushion)/FundedLadderStep)` = 1 + floor(cushion/S)
  with the recipe values; size fixed at the session start from the day-start cushion, same as `fu_ladder_m`; the bust check
  `bal + FL[g,t] <= thr` (intraday low vs the EOD-set threshold) matches EOD-trailing liquidation. At B the cushion is (K-1)S + extra, so
  withdrawals never push the size below K (no payout-driven oscillation; only losses step it down).
- Risk cap: research `risk = |e - sl0| x $2 x w` per base contract matches `TooBig` (stop x PV x qty / Qty()).
- Masks: history T1 `st + H (+5*NSEAT) < d24`, T2 `st >= d24` (and `+5*NSEAT <= n` in seats_plan) keep every seat of a plan inside its
  period; bootstrap seat j starts at position 5j of the shared sequence (correlated seats, paired across plans).
- Paths: realized P&L lands at the exit minute; open adverse excursion per minute uses bar extremes (conservative); `conflict_filter` only
  uses trades already open at the new entry's minute; no UltraR trade lost for a missing entry price (0 of 7,671). No lookahead found.
