NQMaster Operating System

How the whole system is meant to run: what trades, how big, on which accounts, what protects it, and what it does by itself when the market changes. Every number below comes from a test on real futures data, run with $1.90 commission plus 1 tick of slippage per side.

NT backtest 2024-01 → 2026-10: 3,072 trades · WR 65.5% · PF 1.52 · $2,385/mo per contractmatched to research: 92.7% of trades
The design

Six layers, each answers one question

L1

Edge: what to trade

Profile Ultra: 19 small, mostly independent strategies (modules) on MNQ, about 6 trades a day, spread around the clock.

night: NF05 · LF0430 · LF06 · REV06 · ON07 · LON open: VOLB · ICT · ORB60 · ORB90 · MOM1030 · CPI09 midday: MOM11 · CRT11 · MSEQ · MSEQS · VW13 · MOM13 late: LATE15 ENG10 · LATEFH

GoldMaster Robust on MGC is the diversifier (correlation with NQ ≈ 0). Edge per trade is at its tested ceiling: 53,000+ mined configurations, ML, order flow, options OI and feature filters added nothing robust.

L2

Size: how much on each trade

One base contract per module. Fixed boosts that held in all four test periods (2010-14, 2015-19, 2020-23, 2024-26):

MOM1030 ×4CPI09 ×4ICT ×2late confluence ×2VW13 2nd lot

In a 50K evaluation the two ~80% win-rate modules go to ×8 new (preset does it). Funded accounts stay at ×4.

L3

Account: where it runs

Only firms that allow bots: MyFundedFutures, Lucid, Bulenox, FundedNext. Apex (since 2026-03) and Topstep don't. One preset per account type sets target, drawdown, consistency, profit stop, risk cap and payouts.

L4

Protection: when not to trade

FOMC days skippedATR < 150 pts: no tradesrisk cap $300 while cushion is lowmax open contractsroll guardhalf-day sessionsaccount sync guardstale-order watchdognews blackout (MFFU)
L5

Adaptation: what happens when the market changes new

The edge monitor (a CUSUM of daily results vs the backtest) raises an alarm when live trading stops looking like the backtest. It never fired in 2020-26; it fired 9 times in 2010-19, when the edge was weak.

On an alarm, NQMaster now switches off for one year (252 trading days) the modules that failed the 5-year review, then returns to the full profile. Pausing everything instead was tested and is worse.

L6

Feedback: what you look at new

Module health report in the Output window (each module's win rate, PF and $ for the whole run and the last 365 days), the chart panel, the log file and alerts. The report is for review, not for sizing: a module's hot year does not predict its next trades.

Operating routine

What you do, and when

Once, before going live

F5 compile on the PC. Strategy Analyzer Ultra 1 contract over 2024-01 → today; send me the trades CSV and I compare it trade by trade with research.

Every day

Nothing manual. Glance at the chart panel and the alerts: stale order, account sync, roll date, edge monitor percentage.

Every month

Read the module health report. Compare live $ per contract with the expected band: a losing month happens in about 1 of 6 months, so only a pattern matters.

At each quarterly roll

When the roll guard warns (8 days before expiry), move the strategy to the next MNQ contract with the same settings.

Every January

Ask me to re-run the 5-year module review (robust16.py) and update the built-in fallback table. Do not switch modules off by hand unless the monitor fires.

On an edge alarm

Automatic: one year on the reviewed module set, then full profile. Your part: no new accounts during the fallback; if the fallback year is also negative per contract, stop and review with me.

Rejected today

Big size on any module that won ≥ 80% over the last year: those trades average +$46 per win vs −$132 per loss (PF 1.34), and at 8-10 contracts busts reach 42-57%. Pausing on an edge alarm: lower Sharpe than doing nothing.

Still the main risk

The edge is a post-2020 regime: before 2020, Ultra's cost-normalised PF was about 1.0-1.2. Range-bound, high-volatility months (like summer 2026) can bust a $2,000 drawdown. The fallback and several accounts are the answer, not more size.
