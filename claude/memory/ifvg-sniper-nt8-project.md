Federico wants an automated MNQ (1-minute) NinjaTrader 8 strategy that passes prop-firm evaluations fast. He originally set $100 risk/trade. He prefers higher win rate and 2R targets, and dislikes 1 trade/day and 200-day pass times.

Research done 2026-09-27 on 2020-01→2026-09 Nasdaq-100 1m data. The strategy that survived is NYOpeningRangeTrend.cs v2 ("NQ Trend Day"). Its bias is prior RTH close vs SMA20.
- ORB module: 60-min range, stop cap 0.2×ATRd, 2R, max 2/day.
- VWAP-pullback module: extension 0.1 ATRd, limit follows VWAP until 14:30, stop 0.2 ATRd, 2R.
- Results: 4.1 trades/week, WR 47%, PF 1.36 (OOS 1.25). 2025 was flat.
- Prop 50K: $200 risk → 83% eventual pass, median 91 days.

Later request: WR >= 70% and $150/day on 4 of 5 days. Mean reversion (840 configs) reached 65-81% WR but every variant lost money out of sample. The validated edge with a 0.5R target gives ~71% WR (PF ~1.19 OOS), and 0.5R is now the default. That only makes ~$12/day at $300 risk, so $150/day would need 4-5 copied funded accounts on the 2R version.
